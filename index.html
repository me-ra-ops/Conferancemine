About ICCSC 2026

LBS Institute of Technology for Women, established in 2001, is Kerala's first engineering college for women. Managed by LBS Centre for Science and Technology, it offers UG and PG programs in engineering and is a recognized research center under KTU. ICCSC 2026, organized by the Departments of ECE & CSE, aims to promote innovations in intelligent computing and communication.

  • Date: January 28–30, 2026
  • Venue: LBS Institute of Technology for Women, Trivandrum
